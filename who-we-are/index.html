About Gashi-k9 Home

At Gashi-k9 Home, we believe that a well-trained dog isn't just a companion-it's family, protector, and partner. Based in Glogovac, Kosovo, we specialize in breeding and training elite working dogs, especially Belgian Malinois, with exceptional drive, loyalty, and balanced temperaments.

Whether for personal protection, family companionship, or professional duty, our dogs are prepared with care, discipline, and love-ready to serve at the highest level. We take pride in seeing them thrive in new homes across Europe and beyond.

From our home to yours, we ensure every K9 is matched with the right environment to grow, protect, and be loved.
